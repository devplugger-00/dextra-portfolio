    03 · About

    One studio. One designer. Your idea.

    DEXTRA is an independent creative design studio founded by Dev. With a focus on creativity, visual storytelling and thoughtful design, DEXTRA transforms ideas into impactful visual and digital experiences.

    Dev is the founder and the designer behind every project. You talk to the person who makes the work, so briefs stay clear and feedback goes straight to the source. The studio works with individuals, creators, startups and businesses.

    • Philosophy

      A design should say one thing clearly. Every choice of shape, colour and word either supports that message or goes.

    • Approach

      Start from the idea and the audience, explore a few real directions, then refine one with care until the details hold up at every size.

    • Storytelling

      Good visuals carry a small story: who it is for, what it promises and why someone should look twice.
